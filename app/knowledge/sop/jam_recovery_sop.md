# SOP - Transfer Zone Jam Recovery

## Scope
Use when transfer station timeout or object-not-arrived alarms occur.

## Safety
- Do not put hands into moving area without proper stop condition
- Follow site lockout/tagout policy where applicable

## Steps
1. Confirm station is in safe state
2. Check whether workpiece is stuck or tilted in transfer zone
3. Remove obstruction following safety policy
4. Clean photoelectric sensor lens and verify indicator LED
5. Reset alarm
6. Retry one cycle only
7. Escalate maintenance if alarm repeats
